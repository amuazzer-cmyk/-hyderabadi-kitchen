Our Story

The Origin of Biryani

The culinary history of Hyderabad is unmatched by any princely state of India. What began as dishes born of necessity to feed marching armies were slowly elevated, in the kitchens of the Nizams, into a fine gourmet cuisine — expensive, aromatic, and unforgettable. To this day, no visitor leaves Hyderabad without a date with its ubiquitous biryani.

Since 1980, Hyderabadi Kitchen has carried that legacy to Oman — a landmark destination for take-away, private catering, home delivery, and feasts fit for kings.
